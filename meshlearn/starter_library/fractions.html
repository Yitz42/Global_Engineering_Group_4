Fractions: the basics

A fraction describes part of a whole. In 3/4, the bottom number (4) says how many equal parts the whole is cut into. The top number (3) says how many of those parts you have.

Picture it

Cut a flatbread into 4 equal pieces and eat 3 of them. You ate 3/4 of the bread. One piece, 1/4, is left.

Equal fractions

1/2, 2/4 and 4/8 are the same amount. Multiply or divide the top and bottom by the same number and the value does not change.

2/4 = (2 ÷ 2) / (4 ÷ 2) = 1/2

Adding fractions

When the bottom numbers match, add the tops and keep the bottom:

1/5 + 2/5 = 3/5

When they do not match, first rewrite them with the same bottom number:

1/2 + 1/4 = 2/4 + 1/4 = 3/4

Fractions and decimals

Divide the top by the bottom to get a decimal: 1/2 = 0.5 and 3/4 = 0.75. In many countries the decimal point is written as a comma: 0,75.

Try it

  1. What fraction of a week is one day?
  2. Write 6/8 in its simplest form.
  3. What is 1/3 + 1/3?

Answers: 1/7 · 3/4 · 2/3
