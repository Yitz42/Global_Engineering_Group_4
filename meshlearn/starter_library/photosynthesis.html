How plants make food: photosynthesis

Plants do not eat. They make their own food from light, water and air. This process is called photosynthesis.

What goes in

  • Light, usually from the sun.
  • Water, pulled up from the soil through the roots.
  • Carbon dioxide, a gas taken from the air through tiny holes in the leaves.

What comes out

  • Sugar (glucose), which the plant uses for energy and to grow.
  • Oxygen, released into the air. Animals and people breathe it.
carbon dioxide + water + light energy → sugar + oxygen

Where it happens

Inside leaf cells are small green parts called chloroplasts. They contain chlorophyll, the green substance that captures light. That is why most leaves are green.

Why it matters

Almost every food chain starts with photosynthesis. The maize, beans or rice on your plate stored the sun's energy as sugar and starch while they grew.
